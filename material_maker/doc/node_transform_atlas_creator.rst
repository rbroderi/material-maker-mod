Atlas Creator node
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The **Atlas Creator** node: creates an atlas from up to 4 images (2x2, or 4x1 in a row).

Inputs
++++++

* **Image 1**: Image 1 of the atlas
* **Image 2**: Image 2 of the atlas
* **Image 3**: Image 3 of the atlas
* **Image 4**: Image 4 of the atlas

Outputs
+++++++

* **Output**: The atlas

Parameters
++++++++++

* **Layout**: The layout of the atlas

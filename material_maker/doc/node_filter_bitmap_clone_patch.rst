Clone Patch node
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The **Clone Patch** node: copies a circular patch of an image over another area, with soft edges (to remove blemishes from photos).

Inputs
++++++

* **Input**: The image

Outputs
+++++++

* **Output**: The image with the cloned patch

Parameters
++++++++++

* **Source X**: The center of the copied patch
* **Source Y**: The center of the copied patch
* **Target X**: The center of the area that is replaced
* **Target Y**: The center of the area that is replaced
* **Radius**: The radius of the patch
* **Softness**: The softness of the patch's edge, relative to its radius
